Use case: ui-mockup.
Generate a NEW, beautiful high-fidelity UI concept image for URBAN BLADE, a premium mobile grooming, salon booking and product shopping app. This is DESIGN CONCEPT 02, a client approval preview, not implemented software. The previous black-and-amber design was rejected. COMPLETELY change the color mood to luminous PEARL WHITE, soft SEAFOAM, translucent AQUA, DEEP TEAL typography and silver-glass details. NO amber, gold, orange, tan, cream/beige, purple, or black navigation bars.

Style direction: inspired by Apple's iOS 26 Liquid Glass: optically convincing translucent floating capsule controls; very subtle refraction at rounded glass edges; softly diffused background color visible through frosted glass; delicate white specular strokes, generous radii, native-feeling segmented controls, soft depth. Use glass primarily for navigation, toolbars and sticky controls. Content itself stays beautifully readable, airy and calm. SF Pro-like clean modern sans serif, elegant bold headlines, large clear touch targets. A sophisticated wellness and grooming product, not a futuristic neon dashboard. Minimal shadows, precise alignment, restrained use of color, no decorative bubbles or random glass shapes.

Composition: a high-resolution LANDSCAPE presentation with THREE LARGE straight-on iPhone interface screens side-by-side, flat elevation, identical size, all perfectly aligned. Make these three screens large enough to read without zooming. Quiet pale pearl studio background with a very faint seafoam light wash. Minimal thin silver device frames, tiny realistic iOS status bar and dynamic island. Small restrained header at the top of the board: "URBAN BLADE" and "Concept 02 — Pearl & Seafoam". Each screen has a small label above: "HOME", "PRODUCT", "BOOKING". No perspective, no rotating phones, no architectural staging, no giant promotional type outside the screens. Screens should fill almost the whole image.

Palette: background #F4F8F7, white cards #FFFFFF, pale seafoam #DDEFE8, icy aqua #DAEEEF, dark ink-teal #123B39 for text, main action #246B61, muted text #6A807B, selected tint #CCE4DC. Navigation is transparent milky GLASS, never black. Most surface area is pearl-white; rich teal is used only on meaningful text and primary actions. Sharp contrast and legibility.

Constraints: three distinct fully finished SCREENS, not wireframes. All gutters, icon weights, typography, rounded corners, button geometry and semantic color hierarchy are consistent. Keep photographic imagery realistic and tasteful. Glass should visibly transmit softly blurred content but never undermine readability. Show a genuinely fresh iOS 26-inspired design direction that is different in color AND material from the earlier heavy black/amber storefront. Render all key text legibly and avoid excessive microcopy.
