Use case: ui-mockup.
Create a visually outstanding NEW mobile shopping app UI concept board for URBAN BLADE, a grooming products + salon brand. This is DESIGN CONCEPT 03 for client approval. The previous quiet spa/seafoam editorial designs were rejected. This iteration must look like a modern, compelling, feature-rich commerce app, taking information architecture inspiration from the current Amazon and Flipkart shopping experience: prominent search, delivery context, browsable categories, useful offer modules, strong product cards, unmistakable prices and purchase actions. Combine that shopping usefulness with very convincing iOS 26 LIQUID GLASS controls. Do NOT copy Amazon or Flipkart logos or pretend to be either company.

Make ONE high-resolution landscape presentation with FOUR large perfectly front-facing phone screens, aligned and equally sized, with enough space to see every screen clearly: HOME, COLLECTION, PRODUCT, BAG. Small quiet header "URBAN BLADE" and "Concept 03 / Liquid Commerce". Small footer "Motion direction: spring tabs · card expansion · frosted sheets · add-to-bag". This is a static UI preview; the footer communicates proposed motion, not a claim that the image is animated. Screens fill almost all of the composition. Thin silver phone frames. Soft neutral pearl-gray board, no 3D camera angle, no tabletop staging, no busy background.

A FRESH COLOR SYSTEM: icy blue-white #F3F7FC, rich ink navy #102340 text, confident cobalt #245DF5 primary CTAs, very pale cool blue #E5EFFF, subtle pistachio/citron #DFEDAC offer accents, white product surfaces. Restrained purposeful color, not neon. No purple gradients. Do not use the prior black-and-gold or green spa palette. Professional crisp SF Pro-like sans-serif text. Rounded 24px modules, organized 12px gutters, clean tiny category labels, clear price hierarchy, premium editorial product photos.

LIQUID MATERIAL, noticeably more than an ordinary flat white UI: translucent glass floating search capsules, softly refractive circular toolbar buttons, luminous glass bottom dock with milky white edge highlights and gently transmitted blurred card colors below, tactile glass segmented filters, softly blurred layered buy/cart action tray. The glass effect must have optically convincing depth and edge lighting, not generic drop shadows or neon outlines. Keep dark text very readable. Product photos and text cards are solid and clear; glass lives in controls and navigation. Use a consistent five-item Home / Services / Book / Shop / Account dock on HOME and COLLECTION. Selected tab has a cobalt-tinted glass oval. No overlapping nav labels.

Motion aesthetic communicated through the visual design: gently deforming selected glass capsules, an expanding card-to-product shared surface, a shallow frosted sheet with depth, and a compact added-to-bag pill on the BAG screen, small and tasteful. Do not use motion blur that makes any text unreadable. This image must look like a top-tier finished product designer's actual shopping UI, with attractive high-density card sections and real e-commerce usefulness. Not a generic wellness landing page, not a wireframe, not an abstract glass experiment. Key copy and prices should be accurately spelled, all screens consistent and completely visible.
