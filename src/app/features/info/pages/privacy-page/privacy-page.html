Privacy policy

How Urban Blade uses data when you shop hair care, beard, skin, tools, and gift cards.

What we collect

  • Account name, email, and saved delivery addresses for checkout.
  • Order details — products, quantities, and payment method you choose.
  • Your list and recently viewed product IDs stored in this browser.
  • Support messages if you email or call about an order.

How we use it

  • To deliver hair, beard, skin, tool, and gift-card orders.
  • To show your list, recently viewed items, and account addresses.
  • To answer product support — missing packs, damaged goods, or gift-card codes.
  • We do not sell your data to other brands.

Cookies and local storage

This site keeps your cart, account, addresses, list, and recently viewed items in your browser so the store works the next time you visit. Clearing site data removes them.

Sharing

We share only what a delivery partner needs to ship a parcel, or what payment needs to complete checkout. Product support may look up an order ID you send us.
